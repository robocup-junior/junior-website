## The People Behind RoboCupJunior

RoboCupJunior is a global community driven by the hard work and passion of many dedicated individuals. Our mission to inspire young people in STEM is made possible by the incredible efforts of our committees, executives, and trustees. This page is dedicated to celebrating the people who lead and shape the RoboCupJunior community.

### Executive Committee and Trustees

The RoboCup Federation Trustees and the Executive Committee provide strategic leadership and guidance for RoboCup. The members of these boards who represent RoboCupJunior are responsible for overseeing the committees, global operations and growth. They are responsible for steering our organization and ensuring our long-term vision is realized.

## International Volunteer Community

Around the world there is an extensive RoboCup community who dedicate their time to growing RoboCup in their own regions. RoboCupJunior has an extensive network of RoboCup volunteers that come each year to support the running of RoboCupJunior International events. To be part of this network, we highly recommend joining our discord and registering as a potential volunteer to ensure that you are aware of calls for volunteering at SuperRegional and International events.

## Regional Representatives

RoboCupJunior’s global reach is powered by our regional representatives, who organize competitions and support teams in their respective countries. They are the key to building and nurturing the local communities that feed into the international competition.

## Join Our Community

We invite you to become a part of the global RoboCupJunior family. Whether you’re a competitor, mentor, parent, or volunteer, there are many ways to get involved and contribute to our mission.

- **Join the RoboCupJunior mailing list** to stay up-to-date on news and announcements.
- **Visit the RoboCupJunior Forum** to connect with others, ask questions, and share ideas.
- **Join the RoboCupJunior Community Discord** to connect to current and former RoboCup participants and organizers
