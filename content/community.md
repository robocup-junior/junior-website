## Executive Committee and Trustees {#leadership}

The RoboCup Federation Trustees and the Executive Committee provide strategic leadership and guidance for RoboCup. The members of these boards who represent RoboCupJunior oversee the committees, global operations and growth. They steer our organization and make sure our long-term vision is realized.

{{< about-card title="International Volunteer Community" level="2" url="https://robocup-junior.github.io/soccer-rules/discord/" button="Join the Community Discord" >}}
Around the world, an extensive RoboCup community dedicates its time to growing RoboCup in their own regions. Each year, a network of RoboCup volunteers comes to support the running of RoboCupJunior International events.

To be part of this network, we highly recommend joining our Discord and registering as a potential volunteer. That way you will hear about calls for volunteers at SuperRegional and International events.
{{< /about-card >}}
{{< about-card title="Regional Representatives" level="2" url="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aIRAKW0iF_Pxi4grYXEGVand2XSj1oLw/view" button="List of Regional Representatives (2025–2026)" new-tab="true" >}}
RoboCupJunior’s global reach is powered by our regional representatives, who organize competitions and support teams in their regions.

They are the key to building and nurturing the local communities that feed into the international competition.

## Join Our Community {#join}

We invite you to become a part of the global RoboCupJunior family. Whether you’re a competitor, mentor, parent, or volunteer, there are many ways to get involved and contribute to our mission.

{{< about-grid cols="3" >}}
{{< about-card title="Mailing list" url="https://lists.robocup.org/mm/lists/robocup-junior.lists.robocup.org/" button="Mailing List" >}}
**Join the RoboCupJunior mailing list** to stay up-to-date on news and announcements.
{{< /about-card >}}
{{< about-card title="Forum" url="https://junior.forum.robocup.org/" button="RoboCupJunior Forum" >}}
**Visit the RoboCupJunior Forum** to connect with others, ask questions, and share ideas.
{{< /about-card >}}
{{< about-card title="Discord" url="https://robocup-junior.github.io/soccer-rules/discord/" button="Join the Community Discord" >}}
**Join the RoboCupJunior Community Discord** to connect to current and former RoboCup participants and organizers.
