{{< league-intro logo="/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/logo_onstage.png" logo-alt="RoboCupJunior OnStage logo" >}}
**RoboCupJunior OnStage** invites teams to design, build and program creative, autonomous physical robots. The objective is a live robotic performance that **uses a wide variety of technologies to engage an audience**: dance, storytelling, theatre, games and more. For inspiration, check out the [RoboCupJunior OnStage YouTube channel](https://robocup-junior.github.io/onstage-rules/main/onstage_rules.html#rcj-onstage-channel).

Music is optional. The league is intended to be open-ended, and teams are encouraged to be creative, innovative and entertaining in both the design of their robots and the overall performance.
{{< /league-intro >}}

{{< league-section title="About OnStage" >}}
{{< league-media video="ydLyfI35cJ8" video-title="RoboCupJunior Bordeaux 2023 - OnStage Aftermovie" side="right" >}}
An OnStage performance should use innovative technologies, or use technology in new, previously unexplored ways that add value to the performance. Innovation can be shown through clear evidence of testing, research and development, which may inspire future competitors.

A performance must showcase the implementation and integration of **four** robotic features in ways that visually enhance or add value and contribute to the theme or story. **The four chosen features must be clearly seen by the judges during the performance.**

{{< league-section title="Features in OnStage" tone="panel" >}}
{{< league-media image="/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/DSC05001-405x228.jpg" alt="Teams working on their robots in the OnStage workshop area" size="small" >}}
Teams must highlight four robot **features** in the work they present to the judges, and show how those features are integrated to enhance the performance in unique and innovative ways.

<ul class="lg-list">
<li><strong>Explain your choice:</strong> describe and give reasons for your four features in the Technical Description Poster, the Technical Demonstration Video and the Technical Interview.</li>
<li><strong>Keep them distinct:</strong> similar or overlapping features are scored as a single feature.</li>
<li><strong>Ask if unsure:</strong> for clarification on your team's features, contact the OnStage League committee on the <strong>RoboCupJunior Forum</strong>.</li>
</ul>

More information on choosing features is in the article linked below.
