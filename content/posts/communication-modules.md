## Introducing the RCJ Soccer SuperTeam Communication Module

### A Game-Changer for Referees and Teams

To make RCJ Soccer SuperTeam games more manageable for referees and to provide a robust and simple way for robot-to-robot and robot-to-referee communication, we are excited to introduce this new communication module.

To learn more about the module, see the GitHub repository. If you don’t want to build your own, you can buy a pre-assembled module.
