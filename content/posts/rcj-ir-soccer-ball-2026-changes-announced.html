Soccer Lightweight Ball changes in 2026

Starting in 2026, Lightweight Soccer will be moving to a new IR Ball. The key difference with this ball is the size change from 74mm to 42mm diameter, which is the same size as the Open League’s passive ball.

This ball is Open-Source, so anyone can produce one from the files & instructions on the GitHub Page. If you wish to buy a pre-assembled ball, the link is below:
