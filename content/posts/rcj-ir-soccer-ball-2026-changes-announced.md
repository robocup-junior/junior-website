## Soccer Lightweight Ball changes in 2026
{{< /wp-prose >}}

{{< wp-image image="/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/close-1-1024x753.jpg" alt="The new golf-size IR ball held between two fingers, with an inset showing the ball glowing under an infrared camera" figure-class="aligncenter size-large is-resized" wrapper-class="wp-block-image" >}}

{{< wp-prose >}}
Starting in 2026, Lightweight Soccer will move to a new IR ball. The key difference is the size: the diameter changes from 74mm to 42mm, the same size as the Open League’s passive ball.

The ball is open source, so anyone can make one from the files and instructions on the GitHub page. To buy a pre-assembled ball, use one of the links below.
