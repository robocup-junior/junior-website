RoboCup 2027 is heading to Germany! Join us from June 17 to 21, 2027, at the Nuremberg Exhibition Center for the RoboCup world championships, including the RoboCupJunior International Competition.

RoboCup 2027 is hosted by RoboCup Germany. All event details will be published on the official website as they become available.

Visit 2027.robocup.org

Want to Compete at RoboCup 2027?

RoboCupJunior teams qualify for the International Competition through their own region. Talk to your Regional Representative about your region’s qualifying events, and read Qualification and Competitions to find out how the season works.

New to RoboCupJunior? There’s still time to start a team this season. See How to Get Started.

Stay Up to Date

Follow RoboCup Germany on YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn, and join the RoboCupJunior Community Discord for announcements. Questions about the event can go to the organizers at rc27 [@] robocup.de.

See you in Nuremberg!
