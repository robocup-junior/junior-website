**RoboCup 2027 is heading to Germany!** Join us from **June 17 to 21, 2027**, at the **Nuremberg Exhibition Center** for the RoboCup world championships, including the RoboCupJunior International Competition.
{{< /wp-prose >}}

RoboCup 2027 is hosted by RoboCup Germany. All event details will be published on the official website as they become available.

## Want to Compete at RoboCup 2027?

RoboCupJunior teams qualify for the International Competition through their own region. Talk to your **Regional Representative** about your region’s qualifying events, and read [Qualification and Competitions](/qualification/) to find out how the season works.

New to RoboCupJunior? There’s still time to start a team this season. See [How to Get Started](/gettingstarted/).

## Stay Up to Date

Follow RoboCup Germany on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@robocupgermany), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/robocupgermany/) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/robocup-germany), and join the [RoboCupJunior Community Discord](https://robocup-junior.github.io/soccer-rules/discord/) for announcements. Questions about the event can go to the organizers at rc27 [@] robocup.de.

See you in Nuremberg!
