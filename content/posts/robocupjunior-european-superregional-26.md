The RoboCupJunior European Championship 2026 takes place in Vienna from June 3-6, 2026 at Marx Halle. It is co-located with one of the major international robotics conferences, the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation 2026 ([ICRA 2026](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/)), which takes place in Vienna from June 1-5, 2026 at VIECON Messe Wien. This gives participants a unique opportunity to see cutting-edge robotics research, and brings attention to this unique STEM initiative.

Participants at ICRA 2026 will have the opportunity to visit RCJEC2026 on one of the competition days. Guided tours of the event will also be offered for Austrian school classes.
{{< /media-text >}}

{{< wp-prose >}}
- **Opening ceremony, training:** June 3, 2026 (afternoon)
- **Competitions:** June 4 and 5, 2026
- **Awards and closing ceremony:** June 6, 2026 (afternoon)
