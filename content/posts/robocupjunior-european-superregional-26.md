The RoboCupJunior European Championship 2026 takes place in Vienna from June 3-6, 2026 at Marx Halle. It is collocated with one of the major international robotics conferences, the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation 2026 (ICRA 2026, [https://2026.ieee-icra.org/](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/) ), which takes place in Vienna from June 1-5, 2026 at VIECON Messe Wien. That provides a unique opportunity to expose the participants with cutting edge robotics research and to gain attention to promote this unique STEM initiative.

Participants at ICRA 2026 will have the opportunity to visit RCJEC2026 on one of the competition days. Guided tours of the event will also be offered for Austrian school classes.
