Join the Global RoboCupJunior Team in Incheon, South Korea

RoboCupJunior is looking for passionate volunteers from around the world to help deliver the RoboCup 2026 competition in Incheon, South Korea (30–6 July 2026).

Each year, RoboCupJunior brings together hundreds of students, educators, researchers, and robotics enthusiasts from across the globe. Volunteers play a vital role in making the competition possible, supporting teams and helping deliver an inspiring international robotics event.

Whether you are a returning RoboCupJunior volunteer or joining us for the first time, we would love to welcome you to the team.

Applications are now open for international volunteers. 👉 Volunteer applications are available through the RoboCupJunior Volunteer Portal: https://volunteers.junior.robocup.org/

The deadline for priority intake is 30 April 2026. Applications submitted after this date may still be reviewed, however we cannot guarantee they will be processed in time.

We have a tutorial which can assist you with your application

Volunteer Portal Tutorial

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering at RoboCupJunior is a unique opportunity to be part of the world’s largest robotics competition. Volunteers often say that RoboCupJunior is one of the most rewarding events they have ever been involved with.

As a volunteer you will:

  • Help deliver an international robotics competition for students from around the world
  • Support teams and contribute to a positive competition experience
  • Meet robotics researchers, educators, and innovators
  • Connect with the global RoboCup community
  • Gain experience supporting a large international event

Volunteer Roles

We welcome volunteers with a wide range of skills and experience.

Possible roles include:

  • Competition field assistants
  • Technical support for RoboCupJunior leagues
  • Judging and scoring support
  • Team and mentor support
  • Event logistics and coordination
  • Setup and pack-down assistance

Training will be provided for technical volunteer roles where required.

Volunteer Selection Process

Volunteers will be selected through the RoboCupJunior volunteer application system.

Please note the following:

  • Priority is given to volunteers who are available for the entire event
  • Completing the application does not guarantee selection
  • Applicants may be contacted by league committees for further information

If you are new to the international RoboCupJunior community and applying for a technical role, we recommend contacting your regional representative to let them know you are interested.

Final confirmations will be sent by email once the volunteer team has been selected.

Volunteer Support

While volunteers are responsible for their own travel arrangements, RoboCupJunior will provide several forms of support.

Confirmed volunteers will receive:

  • 🎟️ Free registration to RoboCup 2026, including access to the RoboCup Symposium
  • 🎓 Training for RoboCupJunior volunteer roles
  • 🤝 Opportunities to network with researchers, teams, and volunteers from around the world

Volunteers are responsible for covering their own travel costs, including flights and local transport. Please note that accommodation availability is still being confirmed. If you plan to volunteer, we recommend booking refundable accommodation until you receive confirmation.

Event Schedule

The key dates for RoboCupJunior volunteers are:

June 30
Setup Day and Volunteer Training (afternoon)

July 1
Opening Ceremony

July 2–4
Competition Days

July 5
Final Competition Day and Closing Ceremony

July 6
RoboCup Symposium (presentations and networking)

Volunteers are strongly encouraged to be available for the entire event period.

How to Apply

Applications are submitted through the RoboCupJunior Volunteer Portal.

👉 https://volunteers.junior.robocup.org/

You will need to create an account in order to access the application form. After submitting your application, the RoboCupJunior Executive Committee will review all submissions and contact applicants via email. Please allow several weeks for this process.

Questions?

If you have questions about volunteering with RoboCupJunior, please contact the RoboCupJunior Executive Committee: junior_execs [at] lists.robocup.org

See You in Incheon!

RoboCupJunior would not be possible without the incredible volunteers who support the competition each year.

We hope to welcome volunteers from across the global community to Incheon, South Korea in July 2026.

Join us in inspiring the next generation of robotics innovators.
