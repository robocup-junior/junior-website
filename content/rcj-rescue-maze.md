{{< league-intro tagline="Come prepared to be the most successful rescue team." >}}
In **RoboCupJunior Rescue Maze**, the robot searches a maze for victims. Instead of finding the fastest path, it should explore as much of the maze as possible. The robot scores points for each victim found and for delivering a rescue kit close to the victim, and it must avoid areas with black floors.
{{< /league-intro >}}

{{< league-facts label="Rescue Maze at a glance" >}}
{{< league-fact label="Age" >}}**14 to 19** years (age as of 1 July){{< /league-fact >}}
{{< league-fact label="Robot" >}}Fully autonomous, with no human assistance{{< /league-fact >}}
{{< league-fact label="Goal" >}}Explore the maze, find victims and deliver rescue kits{{< /league-fact >}}
{{< league-fact label="Avoid" >}}Areas with black floors{{< /league-fact >}}
{{< /league-facts >}}

{{< league-section title="The mission" >}}
{{< league-media image="/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/53667960461_ee7041312e_o-1024x683.jpg" alt="Rescue Maze robot moving between white maze walls" >}}
<p class="lg-quote">The land is too dangerous for humans to reach the victims. Your team has been given a difficult task. The robot must carry out a rescue mission in a fully autonomous mode with no human assistance.</p>

{{< league-more summary="Read the full scenario" >}}
*The robot must be durable and intelligent enough to navigate treacherous terrain with hills, uneven land and rubble without getting stuck. The robot must search for victims, dispense rescue kits, and signal the position of the victims so the humans can take over. Time and technical skills are essential!*
{{< /league-more >}}
