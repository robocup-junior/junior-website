{{< wp-intro >}}*Guides, code, practice fields and talks from the RoboCupJunior community*{{< /wp-intro >}}

{{< wp-prose >}}
Since RoboCupJunior began in 2000, teams, mentors and organizers around the world have shared what they have learned. This library brings it together: the current rules for every league, beginner tutorials, open-source code and simulators, plans for building your own fields, and talks and papers from the Workshop on Educational Robotics (WEROB).

New to RoboCupJunior? Start with [Starting a Team](/robocup-beginners-guide/).
{{< /wp-prose >}}

{{< wp-anchored-heading id="library" text="Find a Resource" >}}

{{< wp-prose >}}
Choose your league, the kind of help you need and your level, or search by topic, year or author. Rules and guides link to their current versions, so they are always up to date.
{{< /wp-prose >}}

{{< wp-anchored-heading id="share" text="Share Your Work" >}}

{{< wp-prose >}}
Know a great guide, video or open-source project that should be here? [Suggest a resource](https://github.com/robocup-junior/rcj-resources/issues/new?template=suggest-resource.yml) using the short form on GitHub. Once a maintainer approves it, it appears here automatically.

The library is maintained in the open in the [rcj-resources repository](https://github.com/robocup-junior/rcj-resources), where you can also report a broken link. No GitHub account? Share it on the [RoboCupJunior Forum](https://junior.forum.robocup.org/) or email us at junior_execs \[@\] lists.robocup.org.
{{< /wp-prose >}}

{{< wp-anchored-heading id="werob" text="Workshop on Educational Robotics (WEROB)" >}}

{{< wp-padded-prose >}}
The RoboCupJunior Workshop on Educational Robotics has been held alongside the RoboCup Symposium since 2010. Teachers, mentors and students present their research, practice and robots, and run hands-on workshops. Find out how to take part on the [WEROB page](/rcj-werob/).
